How it works

  1. Send your plans

    Upload your home plans and plot plan with the project address. That's all we need to get started.

  2. We work out the requirements

    We check which reports your building department wants and which energy code it enforces, then confirm the work and price with you.

  3. You get your reports

    We prepare the reports and send them to you, ready for your permit application.

What we prepare

Residential

REScheck
Shows that a home's walls, ceilings, floors, windows and doors meet the local energy code.
Manual J
Calculates how much heating and cooling a home needs, based on its size, construction and climate.
Manual S
Matches the HVAC equipment to the Manual J result, so the system is the right size for the load.

Commercial

COMcheck
Shows that a commercial building's envelope, lighting and mechanical systems meet the energy code.

About Mark

Mark Osborne is an environmental and energy efficiency consultant for residential and commercial construction, with 10+ years of experience. He offers fast and accurate REScheck creation for energy efficiency code compliance.

Questions

How do I know which reports I need?

You don't have to. We review your plans, check your building department's requirements and tell you what your project needs.

Do I need to know which energy code applies?

No. We find that out from your project address.

What if my plans are missing some details?

Send what you have. If we need anything else, we'll ask you.

What if my building department needs a stamped calculation?

Some jurisdictions require a licensed engineer's stamp. If yours does, we'll tell you when we confirm the work.

Is permit approval guaranteed?

No. Your building department makes that decision.
